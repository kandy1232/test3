About AqwaGel

A Legacy of Quality & Innovation in Education

Our Story

AqwaGel is a fully owned subsidiary of Artyhomes Pvt Ltd, a company that has been a prominent leader in Sri Lanka's wallpaper manufacturing and interior design industry since 2002.

With over 24 years of expertise in high-quality digital printing, Artyhomes recognized the growing need for educational tools that could engage children beyond screens. Thus, AqwaGel was born — a dedicated educational brand committed to creating innovative learning materials.

Our mission is simple: to provide high-quality educational posters and tools that reduce children's screen addiction and promote cognitive growth.
